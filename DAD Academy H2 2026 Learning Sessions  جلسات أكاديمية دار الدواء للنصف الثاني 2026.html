Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear expectations help every colleague get the most from each learning opportunity.

Registration Deadlines+

Registration operates on a first-come, first-served basis. Early registration is highly encouraged to secure your spot in popular programs.

Attendance Policy+

Full attendance is required. If circumstances prevent attendance, notify L&D immediately to allow waitlisted colleagues to participate.

Manager Approval+

Manager approval is mandatory before registration. Discuss operational coverage and learning objectives with your supervisor first.

Certificates+

Completion certificates are issued after full attendance and any required assessments or follow-up activities are submitted.

Course Limit+

Register for only one course per quarter to allow colleagues to participate and maximize your learning benefits.
